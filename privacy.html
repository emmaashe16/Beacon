Privacy policy

Beacon Survival Kit · Effective 7 October 2026

The short version. Beacon has no accounts and no servers that store your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to send an SOS message yourself.

Who we are

Beacon Survival Kit (“Beacon”, “we”) is an emergency app made by an independent developer based in Ireland. If you have a question about this policy or your data, email YOUR-EMAIL@example.com.

We never receive, see, sell or share any of this. Beacon has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Location

Beacon asks for your location only when you tap Get my location, and explains why before your phone asks for permission. It does not track you in the background. You can turn location access off at any time in your phone's settings.

Camera

Beacon uses your camera only when you tap Take a photo. If your phone supports it, Beacon may also use the camera's flashlight when you turn on the light. Photos are never uploaded.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to send it:

  • SOS messages. Tapping Text opens your own messaging app with the message filled in. You decide whether to send it. Your mobile carrier or messaging service handles delivery under its own privacy policy.
  • Calls. Tapping Call opens your phone's dialler.
  • Map links. SOS messages include a Google Maps link with your coordinates. Google receives the location only if the person you sent it to opens that link.

Services that load the app

  • GitHub Pages hosts Beacon's files. Like any web host, it may log your IP address when the app downloads. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the app's typefaces, so Google may receive your IP address when fonts load. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Play, if you installed Beacon from there, handles downloads under Google's own policy.

None of these services receive your contacts, medical info, location or photos from Beacon.

Legal basis (GDPR)

Medical details are special category data and location is sensitive data. Because Beacon processes them only on your device, at your request, for your own safety, we rely on your explicit consent. You give it by entering the information or tapping Get my location, and you can withdraw it at any time by deleting the data.

Deleting your data

Open the SOS tab and tap Delete all my data at the bottom. This removes your contacts, medical info, location and photos from your phone. Uninstalling Beacon or clearing your browser's website data also removes it. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, and to complain to a supervisory authority. In Ireland that is the Data Protection Commission. Since your data stays on your phone, you can view, change or delete all of it directly in the app.

Children

Beacon is not aimed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Not a replacement for emergency services

Beacon is a helper, not an emergency service. Always call 112, 999 or your local emergency number when you can. Messages depend on your phone signal and may not be delivered.
